Every change to Winlux goes through our automated tests. During those tests, a series of screens is captured: Settings, Start menu, taskbar panels, in-house apps. This page shows the screenshots of the latest runs, newest first, with the test that took each image. It updates itself after every run.

> [!note]
> Only Winlux's own screens are published here, picked from a fixed list and checked automatically before publication. Tests of other publishers' software stay private.
