## What Winlux does for you

::: cards
### A double-click is enough
A downloaded `.exe` opens like any other file, from the desktop or from Explorer. `setup.exe` and `.msi` installers add their shortcut to the Start menu.

### A familiar desktop
Start menu centred or on the left, taskbar, Explorer, Settings, Task Manager: you find your way around from the very first minute.

### Your files, everywhere
Your home folder is the `C:` drive for Windows programs. Documents, Pictures and Downloads are the same for all your applications.

### Built in-house
Windows compatibility is written from scratch, in Rust, for Winlux. A single system runs on your PC: no Windows hidden in a virtual machine.

### Your privacy
Local accounts only, no telemetry. A program asks for your consent before using the camera, the microphone or your personal folders.

### Four languages
The system, the installer and this website are available in French, English, German and Spanish.
:::

## Where the project stands

Winlux is a **technical preview**: the desktop, the installer, updates and many 64-bit Windows programs work, but not every piece of software opens yet. 32-bit programs, recent Direct3D 11 games and .NET desktop applications (WinForms, WPF) are among the work in progress. The full list is kept up to date in the [wiki](wiki:Limites-et-problemes-connus).

This website is for people discovering Winlux. To understand how it is built, read the [technical wiki](wiki:Home) (in French).
