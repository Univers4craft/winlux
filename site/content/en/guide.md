## Before you start

> [!warning]
> Winlux is a technical preview. Before installing it on a real PC, **back up your documents** to another medium, and preferably try it in a virtual machine first.

You will need a 64-bit PC with 4 GB of memory and 24 GB of disk space, a USB drive of 4 GB or more, and about half an hour. The details are on the [Download](page:download#what-you-need) page.
